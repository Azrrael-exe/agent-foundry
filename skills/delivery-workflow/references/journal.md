# Implementation journal and current technical documentation

Maintain a concise decision history and an accurate current technical view for the initiative. Reuse existing project documentation, decision records, and OpenSpec conventions when they already serve these purposes; link to them instead of duplicating them. Otherwise, adapt the [journal](../assets/journal.md) and [implementation](../assets/implementation.md) templates. A suggested location is `docs/initiatives/<id>/`; follow the repository's established location and naming conventions. A small initiative may combine both sections in one record.

Record a decision when it materially affects architecture, contracts, dependencies, tradeoffs, or operational limits. Do not record every action or copy source code. Assign a stable, unique `DEC-...` identifier; include context, the choice, alternatives actually considered, consequences, state, affected component references, and supporting evidence. Label unknowns and assumptions explicitly. A record documents a decision; it does not grant approval or authorize implementation.

Keep prior decisions available when circumstances change. Mark a replaced decision superseded, link it to its successor by ID, and have the successor link back and state the trigger for replacement. Do not silently rewrite history. Identify proposed or unresolved decisions as such, and distinguish intended behavior from observations and evidence. Never include secrets.

After an increment, update the current technical view for the changed components: responsibilities, interfaces and contracts, data flow, dependencies and reusable components, operation or run instructions, and known limits. Identify real and simulated integrations, and link to authoritative code, contracts, configuration, and verification evidence rather than reproducing them. Mark uncertain or unverified behavior and stale details honestly. Keep historical decisions in the journal; keep the current view focused on what is true now.
