# Work agreement

Use an agreement when coordinating an initiative under this skill. Adapt the
[agreement template](../assets/agreement.md) to the work; record only what is
known, and resolve material gaps before taking the affected action. Link the
approved spec and evidence of its approval, or record that approval is still
pending. Keep the agreement and its changes identifiable alongside the work.

Record work type (product, feature, or prototype), current and target maturity
(exploration, demo, pilot, or production), and execution mode independently.
These describe intent, not authority: neither maturity nor staged or continuous
mode grants permission. Record the actions already authorized, their evidence,
protected boundaries, and consultation triggers. Existing approvals persist;
do not ask again for actions already authorized. Follow the user's instructions,
project conventions, sandbox permissions, and adapter trust rules when they
constrain the agreement.

In staged mode, retain the existing spec-builder handoff: an approved spec can
be handed off for implementation without opting into continuous execution. In
continuous mode, proceed only within the recorded authorization and stop or
consult when a boundary or trigger applies. Neither mode creates a mandatory
workflow for other consumers.

Define a delivery condition and stopping conditions before execution. Unless an
explicit retry budget overrides it, stop after two consecutive repair attempts
for the same failure without new evidence or progress. Report the failure and
evidence rather than retrying indefinitely. Revise the agreement when scope,
authorization, or a consequential decision changes; preserve the evidence for
earlier decisions. These instructions coordinate work and are not security
controls or an executable runner.
